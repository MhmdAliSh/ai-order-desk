# Portfolio demo script

## 90-second walkthrough

1. Sign in as the shop owner and open **Messages**. Explain that customers contact the shop through message channels rather than a customer portal.
2. Select a fictional customer message and choose **Review with AI**. Show that the assistant extracts products and quantities, but the employee can correct every suggestion.
3. Save the result as a draft order. Open **Orders**, approve it to reserve stock, then dispatch it to deduct stock.
4. Open **Stock history** to show the timestamped audit record.
5. Open **Operations**. Explain the daily report, alert list, sales signals, and owner-approved restock suggestions. Point out that accepting a suggestion does not buy stock automatically.
6. Open **CSV import**, preview `sample-data/product-import-template.csv`, and explain that no catalog data changes until the owner confirms.

## What this demonstrates

- Safe AI-assisted order intake
- Deterministic price and stock calculations
- Human approval for business decisions
- Audit trail and role-based access
- A practical workflow for a small retailer
